# The tuning objective is window-indexed body quality, gated on a saturating diversity floor

## Context & Decision

Tuning `ppo_codesign_single` on the ant is a first broad pass — **no hyperparameter in that config has been jointly tuned**, and the staged `3a`/`3b`/`3c` winners baked into it came from greedy narrow sweeps that could not see interactions. Sweeping that space needs an objective, and the previous one (`rewards/iter`, recovered level) has two problems here.

First, `rewards/iter` is **epoch-indexed**, so a fixed-length tail lands at an arbitrary phase within a resample window once `horizon_length` or `resample_interval` varies (window length in epochs is `max_episode_length ÷ horizon_length`). That forbids sweeping the timing knobs, which this pass wants. `quality/R_mean` is logged once per window and is phase-aligned by construction.

Second, neither metric penalises generator collapse, and `R_mean` **structurally rewards it**: `R_i` is the mean return of env *i* over the window and `R_mean` averages over the sampled body population, so a more diverse population necessarily contains more bad bodies and scores lower. Maximising `R_mean` alone is partly an instruction to stop exploring.

The objective is therefore:

```
score = mean(quality/R_mean, last 5 windows) × min(1, mean(build/div_struct, last 5 windows) / D_floor)
```

Four choices inside that, each load-bearing:

- **A saturating floor, not a product.** `reward × D^alpha` requires a trade rate between reward and diversity that nothing justifies. A floor asserts only "below `D_floor` is degenerate"; above it, ranking is pure reward. It also defuses the tautology that a diversity term tuned alongside `generator.entropy_coef` would otherwise create — above the floor, extra entropy buys exactly zero score and still costs reward.
- **The gate spans the whole RL phase, not the reward tail.** A generator that explored and then committed to the best body it found is succeeding, and its late-run diversity is low for the right reason. The pathology is *never having searched*, which is a whole-phase question. The two halves answer different questions, so their spans deliberately do not align.
- **The gate reads the reward TAIL, not the whole RL phase.** Diversity is not stationary within a run — it decays monotonically as the generator concentrates — so a whole-series mean is dominated by the opening exploration transient and cannot fire on a run that degenerates later. Measured on the calibration anchor: no floor that passes the anchor also penalises a collapsed run, because even at `floor = 365` (the worst anchor seed, i.e. zero clearance) the whole-series mean of 575.6 still saturates the gate at 1.0. The tail is only SAFE because the metric is `div_struct`: the original whole-series choice existed to avoid punishing healthy late commitment, which under `n_modes` is indistinguishable from collapse (both → ~1). Under `div_struct` healthy commitment reads 8.0 and collapse reads ~0, so the tail asks "did it end up degenerate" without the false positive.
- **`build/div_struct`, not `build/n_modes` or `build/n_distinct`.** `div_struct` is the mean pairwise `d_struct` over all sample pairs on the **subtype-collapsed** skeleton — threshold-free, no clustering, and `0.0` exactly when every draw is the same design, so the floor is interpretable without calibration. `n_distinct` counts exact **typed** designs, and per the `free_entropy` finding the skeleton commits while the subtype axis stays free, so subtype jitter alone keeps it near the sample size under total skeleton collapse: across 20 trials × 23 RL windows of the FD/FK study its global minimum was 286/4096 and its per-trial means 2841–4006. It saturates at the TOP. `n_modes` saturates at the BOTTOM and is additionally **density-confounded** — see Considered Options.

## Considered Options

- **Gate on `build/n_modes`** (the original decision, amended after the calibration wave). Rejected on measurement. It is a Hill number over **single-linkage** `d_struct` clusters at τ=1, and single linkage is *transitive*: it merges along 1-edit **chains** rather than ignoring 1-edit differences, which makes it a percolation statistic rather than a spread statistic. Two consequences, both measured. (1) **Sample density moves it without any change in diversity** — drawing 16× more samples from an *identical* distribution drops it 5× (13.50 → 4.48 → 2.64 at M = 256/1024/4096) while `div_struct` moves 1% (5.59 → 5.48 → 5.65). Since this study sweeps parameters that change how concentrated the generator's sampling is, that confound is not incidental. (2) **It saturates at the collapsed end**, compressing a 20× range of real spread into `[1.0, 3.0]`. On the calibration anchor it fell 260× (1595 → 6) while `div_struct` fell 32% (11.9 → 8.0): the population concentrated mildly and did **not** collapse, and only `div_struct` says so. Retained in `noise_metrics` as a diagnostic. Its across-seed CV is 32%, versus 16% for `div_struct` on the same populations — the metric sitting on a percolation threshold is the noisier one.
- **Keep `rewards/iter` + `recovered` and freeze the timing knobs.** Rejected: it has ~2.4× the across-config discrimination of `R_mean`, which matters near a noise floor, but it costs `horizon_length` and `resample_interval` — and this pass is the one that should look at them. `final_frac` does not rescue it: `tune.py:136-139` subtracts `n_pretrain` (a **window** count) from `n` (an **epoch** count), a cap only meaningful for a window-indexed series.
- **Score `R_mean` alone and inspect diversity by hand afterwards.** Rejected: it leaves the sampler free to find a degenerate corner and only reveals it after the budget is spent. The floor costs nothing when nothing goes wrong.
- **Gate on `build/body_diversity` (`N_body_skel`).** Rejected on the doc's own warning — it inflates when independent limbs flip without breaking the common core, which is exactly what `n_modes` fixes. It is also computed from the generator's own per-step entropies, making it the most direct restatement of `entropy_coef`.
- **Gate on `build/mean_limb_diversity`.** Rejected: empty slots contribute `exp H = 1`, so configs emitting more limbs score mechanically higher and the gate becomes partly a limb-count gate.
- **Multi-objective Pareto (NSGA-II) over reward and diversity.** Rejected as disproportionate: `direction=`, `best_trial`, the top-k centre and the `best_params` export in `tune.py` all assume one scalar.
- **A hard gate (`reward` if `D ≥ D_floor` else `0.0`).** Rejected: the cliff gives TPE no gradient near the boundary and scores a trial that missed by 1% identically to full collapse.

## Consequences

- **Studies scored this way are not comparable to any earlier study.** The FD/FK study's `best_params.yaml` was already never adopted and never confirmed; it is superseded rather than compared against.
- **The gate is expected to be inert.** That is the intent — it is insurance for the low-`generator.entropy_coef` and low-`n_pretrain` corners this sweep visits for the first time, not a second objective. If it fires often, `D_floor` is wrong, not the configs.
- **`D_floor = 3`**, set from the 8-seed calibration wave (`quality/R_mean` 11.8, CV 6%; `build/div_struct` tail-5 spanning 5–8, mean 6.8, SD 1.1, CV 16%; `build/n_modes` 575.6, CV 32%). It sits **1.8 SD below the worst anchor seed** and 3.5 SD below the mean, so seed noise never crosses it and saturation contributes exactly zero score variance on healthy configs — clearance is worth more than bite, because a floor the noise straddles scores identical hyperparameters under two different objectives. The ×0.5 half-discount lands at 1.5, inside the measured degenerate band (`div_struct` 1.74 / 0.92 / 0.37 for populations clustered ever tighter around one design) and far below anything the anchor produces. The floor deliberately does **not** price "how much concentration is too much": refusing that trade rate is the point of a saturating floor, and it is a question for the focus stage with real data rather than a number to guess now.
- **`_log_diversity` gains an O(M²) pairwise step per window.** `_dedup` runs first and subtype collapse is what makes it bite, so the cost is seconds against a 1.5–3 h trial — but it is CPU work in the training loop, and ADR-0017 leaves per-trial CPU unbounded across 8 concurrent slots.
- `quality/R_mean` carries `_r_scale`, so its absolute values (~30–70) are on a different scale from `rewards/iter` (~3500). Ranking is unaffected; quoted numbers are not interchangeable.
- **Measured seed noise on the objective is 6%** (8 seeds), below ADR-0018's whole 9–48% band for `R_mean` — a single trial resolves a ~12% difference in true mean, and the top-k centre over ~36 trials resolves ~1%. The FD/FK study's across-config spread was 17% while varying only five aux parameters, so this 18-parameter sweep should clear 6% comfortably; it must still be read against its own spread before the winner means anything.
- **The objective was verified to measure learning rather than selection.** `R_mean` is a population average, so narrowing onto already-good bodies raises it with no improvement in body quality — the exact confound the gate exists to prevent. On the calibration anchor `R_mean` rose 2.6× (4.80 → 12.38) while `div_struct` fell only 32%. Lifting a population mean that far by pruning would require concentrating onto roughly the top decile, which would cost far more than a third of the mean pairwise distance; the distribution shifted up. This check is cheap and should be repeated on the focus stage's anchor.
- **`R_mean` plateaus by roughly window 15 of 24** on the anchor (4.80 → 11.33 → 12.38 across thirds; the second half contributes +9.2%, or 1.5 SD of seed noise). Tail-5 therefore samples a settled level, as `recovered` intends — but the focus stage's plan to double frames should be re-justified against this before it is spent, since the anchor shows little left to gain past the midpoint.
